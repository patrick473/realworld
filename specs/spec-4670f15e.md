# Feature specification for implementing an estimated reading time calculator and display for article content.

## Requirements

- Calculate estimated reading time for each article based on word count and a configurable words-per-minute rate.
- Display the estimated reading time on the article page in a consistent format (e.g., '5 min read').
- Recalculate and update the reading time automatically whenever article content is created, edited, or republished.
- Allow administrators to configure the default reading speed (words per minute) to adjust accuracy.
- Ensure calculation and display processes do not negatively impact page load performance, utilizing caching or background processing where applicable.
- Handle empty or minimal content gracefully by displaying zero or one minute as appropriate.
